

# AIToyz - Parent Company Website

A playful and creative single-page website for **AIToyz** that showcases your AI/Tech products (oplus, maplix, yourel) with full contact form and newsletter functionality.

---

## 🎨 Design Direction

**Playful & Creative Theme:**
- Vibrant color palette with gradients and pops of color
- Fun animations and micro-interactions on scroll and hover
- Unique layouts with overlapping elements and creative shapes
- Modern typography with bold headings
- Engaging visual elements (icons, illustrations, floating shapes)

---

## 📄 Website Sections (Single Page)

### 1. Hero Section
- Eye-catching animated headline introducing AIToyz
- Tagline about being the home of innovative AI products
- Playful animated background elements (floating shapes, gradients)
- Call-to-action button to explore projects

### 2. Projects Showcase
- **3 interactive project cards** for oplus, maplix, and yourel
- Each card includes:
  - Project logo/image
  - Project name and description
  - Key features or highlights
  - Link to visit the project
- Hover animations for an engaging experience

### 3. About / Mission Section
- Brief story about AIToyz and your mission
- What makes your products unique
- Visual elements to keep it engaging

### 4. Team Section
- Team member cards with photos, names, and roles
- Optional social links
- Creative grid or carousel layout

### 5. Newsletter Signup
- Email subscription form
- Engaging copy about staying updated on new projects
- **Connected to backend** to store subscribers

### 6. Contact Form
- Name, email, and message fields
- Form validation and success feedback
- **Connected to backend** to store submissions

### 7. Footer
- Quick navigation links
- Social media icons
- Copyright and branding
